BS Area Studies

Specializations: African Studies | North American Studies | Latin American Studies

Following the successful commencement of classes last semester, our 4-Year Bachelor of Science (BS) degree provides undergraduate students with foundational expertise in diplomatic history, regional political economy, foreign policy, and comparative institutions.

Eligibility: HSSC / FA / FSc / A-Levels (min. 50% marks)
Credit Hours: 130+ Total Credits + Final Year Internship / Research Project
Status: Classes underway since last semester
2-Year MPhil Degree Postgraduate

MPhil in Area Studies

Advanced Interdisciplinary Research Degree

A rigorous 2-year postgraduate program tailored for scholars seeking advanced analytical methodologies in international relations, strategic doctrines, foreign policy formulation, and trans-continental socio-economic developments.

Eligibility: BS / MA / MSc (16 years education) in Social Sciences (min. 2.5 CGPA)
Structure: 2 Semesters Coursework + Independent Research Dissertation
Doctoral Program PhD Research

PhD in Area Studies

Doctoral Research Fellowship

The terminal research degree producing top-tier academics, policy consultants, and strategic analysts. Focuses on original archival exploration, theoretical frameworks, and high-impact publications in international affairs.

Eligibility: MPhil / MS degree (18 years education, min. 3.0 CGPA) + Subject Test
Requirements: Comprehensive Exam + International Publication + Dissertation Defence

Director's Message

Advancing Rigorous Scholarship & Academic Excellence

I hold a deep conviction that knowledge is central to societal progress, informed policymaking, and meaningful global engagement. It is with this understanding that I lead the Area Study Centre for Africa, North and South America, committed to advancing rigorous scholarship, intellectual openness, and responsible academic leadership.

My academic vision is to strengthen interdisciplinary and policy-relevant scholarship that deepens understanding of the historical, political, economic, cultural, and social dynamics of Africa and the Americas.
