Official Mandate

Pakistan's Premier Statutory Research Institute for Regional Studies

Established under the Area Study Centres Act No. XLV of 1975 passed by the Parliament of Pakistan, the Centre is an autonomous statutory institution directly funded by the Federal Ministry of Education through the Higher Education Commission (HEC) and academically affiliated with Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad.

The Centre specializes in contemporary, historical, geopolitical, strategic, socio-economic, and cultural developments across Africa, North America, and South America, equipping scholars, diplomats, and policy analysts with deep analytical perspectives.

Autonomous Governance

Governed by a statutory Board of Governors chaired by the Vice Chancellor of QAU.

Policy Recommendations

Provides strategic advisories and research briefs to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA).

Core Mandates

Strategic Research Scope

  • Interdisciplinary teaching in BS, MPhil, and PhD degree tracks in Area Studies.
  • Bi-annual publication of HEC Recognized Pakistan Journal of American Studies (PAJAS).
  • Host of national seminars, international webinars, and specialized talk series on global geopolitics.
  • Specialized archival library housing books, journals, and policy briefs on Africa and the Americas.

Specializations: African Studies | North American Studies | Latin American Studies

Following the successful commencement of classes last semester, our 4-Year Bachelor of Science (BS) degree provides undergraduate students with foundational expertise in diplomatic history, regional political economy, foreign policy, and comparative political institutions across Africa, North America, and South America.

2-Year Postgraduate Research Degree

MPhil in Area Studies

Advanced Interdisciplinary Regional Research Track

A rigorous 2-year postgraduate degree tailored for scholars seeking advanced analytical methodologies in international relations, strategic doctrines, foreign policy formulation, trans-continental socio-economic developments, and regional security dynamics.

Eligibility Requirements

16 years of education (BS / MA / MSc) in Social Sciences or Humanities with min. 2.5/4.0 CGPA or 2nd Division from HEC recognized university + QAU Entry Test & Interview.

Program Structure

24 Credit Hours Coursework (2 Semesters) followed by a peer-reviewed research dissertation under senior ASC supervisor.

Terminal Doctoral Research Degree

PhD in Area Studies

Doctoral Fellowship & Archival Research Track

The highest academic research degree producing top-tier university faculty, senior policy consultants, and international strategic analysts. Focuses on original archival exploration, theoretical frameworks, and high-impact peer-reviewed publications.

Doctoral Admission Requirements

MPhil / MS in Area Studies or related Social Science discipline with min 3.0/4.0 CGPA + Subject GRE/QAU Subject Test + Defense of Research Proposal.

HEC Degree Graduation Requirements

Coursework, Comprehensive Examination, Dissertation Proposal Approval, International Peer Review, Publication in HEC journal, and Public Defense.

Director's Message

Advancing Rigorous Scholarship & Global Analytical Leadership

I hold a deep conviction that knowledge is central to societal progress, informed policymaking, and meaningful global engagement. It is with this understanding that I lead the Area Study Centre for Africa, North and South America, committed to advancing rigorous scholarship, intellectual openness, and responsible academic leadership.

I am guided by the core values of inclusivity, equality, diversity, academic freedom, and intellectual integrity. Sustainable academic excellence emerges from environments that respect difference, encourage critical thought, and uphold ethical, evidence-based inquiry. These principles shape my approach to research leadership, academic governance, and institutional development.

My academic vision is to strengthen interdisciplinary and policy-relevant scholarship that deepens understanding of the historical, political, economic, cultural, and social dynamics of Africa and the Americas. Through high-quality research, scholarly exchange, and critical dialogue, I seek to contribute to evidence-informed policymaking and support Pakistan's constructive engagement at regional and global levels.

As Director, I remain committed to expanding research capacity, encouraging funded and applied research, supporting postgraduate and doctoral scholars, and fostering sustainable national and international collaborations with universities, research institutions, and academic networks of international standing.

Guided by shared values, strategic leadership, and collective effort, the Area Study Centre will continue to make meaningful contributions to national priorities, global academic discourse, and the advancement of knowledge.

Legal & Institutional Charter

Statutory Framework

Act No. XLV of 1975

Created under parliamentary statute by the Government of Pakistan to foster high-level, specialized research in international regional studies and strategic foreign policy.

Federal HEC Funding

Functions as an autonomous institute funded directly through the Higher Education Commission (HEC) and the Ministry of Federal Education and Professional Training.

QAU Academic Affiliation

Academically tied to the Faculty of Social Sciences at Quaid-i-Azam University, granting accredited BS, MPhil, and PhD degrees with rigorous academic standards.

Regional Divisions

Research Wings Showcase

Division I

African Studies Wing

Researches the African Union (AU), Sub-Saharan economic growth, African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) implications, and Pakistan's 'Look Africa' diplomatic vision.

  • Trade & Economic Corridors
  • AU Peacekeeping & Governance
  • Afro-Asian Security Linkages
Division II

North American Studies Wing

Analyzes US Foreign Policy grand strategies, US-Pakistan strategic dialogues, Canadian multilateral diplomacy, and Transatlantic security dynamics.

  • US Presidential & Congressional Dynamics
  • Canadian Bipolar & Arctic Strategy
  • US-South Asia Strategic Equations
Division III

South & Latin American Wing

Focuses on Latin American political economy, MERCOSUR trade integration, BRICS dynamics in South America, and emerging South-South cooperation models.

  • MERCOSUR & Andean Trade Frameworks
  • BRICS Expansion & Multipolarity
  • Asian-Latin American Bilateralism
